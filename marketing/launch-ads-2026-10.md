## Geo and budget
Start with Rockford plus selected nearby communities or a deliberately chosen ~20-mile radius, set location targeting to "Presence" to focus on people actually in/regularly in the area.
Don't launch nationwide by accident. Separate campaign ad groups for app & loyalty vs websites.
Proposed starter test (not authorized spend): Google Search $15/day, Meta $10/day for 14 days = $350. Revise after owner approves a budget and form conversion tracking is tested.

## Measurement and launch safeguards
1. Confirm the FormSubmit activation email at optimizelocalai@gmail.com.
2. Submit at least one test inquiry in private browsing. Confirm email received with source labels and replying goes to the lead's address.
3. Configure GA4/Google Ads tag or GTM and Meta Pixel where appropriate; use thank-you page and form acceptance event, never treat clicks to Google Calendar as completed bookings.
4. Confirm consent/privacy policy and local landing page on mobile.
5. Test UTM parameters for Google and Meta.
6. Publish only after approval of creative assets, budget, target audience and payment method.
7. In week one, track submitted leads, qualified leads, booked consultations, show rates and proposals; do not optimize solely for clicks.
